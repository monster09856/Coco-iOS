## Full description (max 4000 char)

**Coco - Match Adventure: A colorful puzzle journey across tropical islands!**

Coco invites you on a vast match-3 adventure with our parrot friend across 12 different tropical islands and 240 levels. Swipe colorful birds, line up 3 or more, chain combos, and earn treasures!

**🐦 240 levels, 12 unique islands**
Explore from lush coconut islands to lavender fields, volcanic craters to bamboo forests under the moonlight. Each island has its own theme and star goals.

**💥 Rockets, bombs, and color blasts**
Line up 4 birds → create a ROCKET that clears full rows or columns.
Match a T or L shape → drop a BOMB that wipes a wide area.
Bring together 5 birds → COLOR BLAST removes all birds of one color.

**🥚 Nest and hidden bird collection**
Three eggs await you in the Nest. As you complete specific levels, eggs crack and rare birds hatch — golden, fire, ice, neon, iridescent — race to collect all 12 species!

**🎰 Lucky Wheel + daily rewards**
Spin the wheel daily for gold, boosters, or lives. Daily login rewards give you 7 days of gifts in a row!

**👑 VIP Membership (Monthly)**
Enjoy unlimited lives, 2x gold, and daily bonus rewards. Auto-renews monthly, cancel anytime.

**💛 Smooth play, easy to learn**
- Lives no longer drop on level start — only on a loss
- After 15 consecutive wins lives reset (skill cap)
- Server-time anti-cheat for life regeneration (no clock tricks)

**📱 Design & experience**
- Smooth tropical animations
- Turkish + English language support
- Light/dark theme adaptive
- Tablet supported

**🎯 Distinct features**
- No live PvP, no scene mode — just you, the birds, and the islands
- Playable offline
- Cloud sync (sign in with Google to keep progress across devices)
- Ad config: watch rewarded ads for +3 moves or an extra life
